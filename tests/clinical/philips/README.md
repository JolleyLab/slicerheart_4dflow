# Philips

Placeholder. No case yet.

When a case is contributed, record in `manifest.json`: the export format
(classic DICOM, enhanced multi-frame DICOM, or PAR/REC), how the
velocity-encoded images are identified, the rescale from stored value to
velocity, and the encoding directions and sign convention relative to the
image axes. The layout and schema in `../README.md` apply unchanged.

The Pravdivtseva in vitro datasets (Zenodo 4882572, CC BY 4.0) were acquired
on a Philips 3 T system and provide raw PAR/REC for straight tubes of 2 to
5 mm diameter at 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5 mm with compressed-sensing factors 2.5 to
13, plus decoded velocity fields of an aneurysm model. They are a candidate
for this folder if a clinical Philips case is not available.
